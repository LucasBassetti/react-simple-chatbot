With `speechSynthesis={{ enable: true, lang: 'en' }}`, the chatbot reads each bot message out loud with the speech synthesis of the browser. See [Speech](/react-simple-chatbot/guides/speech/) for more.

<Demo name="speech-synthesis" />

## How it works

- By default, the chatbot says the text of the message.
- Set `metadata.speak` on a step to say a different text. It also supports `{previousValue}`.
- Custom components have no text to read, so `metadata.speak` is the only way to make them speak.
- Browsers may block the speech until you interact with the page, like typing your name.
