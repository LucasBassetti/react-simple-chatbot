With `recognitionEnable`, the submit button shows a microphone while the input is empty. Click it and speak: the chatbot types what you say and sends it when you stop talking. See [Speech](/react-simple-chatbot/guides/speech/) for more.

It needs a browser with `webkitSpeechRecognition`, like Chrome, Edge and other Chromium-based browsers, or Safari, and permission to use the microphone. In other browsers the chatbot works with the keyboard only.
