The chatbot asks for the height and the weight, checks them with a `validator`, then a custom component computes the BMI (body mass index) and its category. See [Custom components](/react-simple-chatbot/guides/custom-components/) for more.

<Demo name="bmi" />

## How it works

- `numberBetween` creates a validator for each step, with its own range.
- `BMIResult` reads the answers with `steps.height.value` and `steps.weight.value`. The values are strings, so they are converted with `Number`.
- `asMessage: true` shows the result inside a bot bubble.
